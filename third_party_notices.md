# Third-Party Components

No third-party libraries, executables, vehicle models, textures or sounds are bundled in this package.

- `_metadata/0.png`, `_metadata/1.png`: promotional images based on screenshots supplied by Jean_Luc_Picard from Transport Fever 3. The cover typography was added with OpenAI ImageGen. The depicted game artwork was not authored by this project; no ownership of that artwork is claimed. Transport Fever 3 and its artwork remain subject to the applicable game and rightsholder terms. Source: https://www.transportfever3.com/
- `content/mod.script.lua` references installed Transport Fever 3 vehicle resources and, when loaded, the official Early Wagons package. These assets are not copied into the mod. Source: https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/early-wagons
- Concept inspiration: nary_believe's Transport Fever 2 mod `vanilla_wagon_speed_variants_1`. This is a separate TF3 implementation; no code or assets from that mod are bundled.
